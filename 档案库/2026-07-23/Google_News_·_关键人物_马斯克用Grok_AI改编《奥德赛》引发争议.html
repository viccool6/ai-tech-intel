In case you missed it, Elon Musk wants to make his own version of Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey using Grok — and Jimmy Kimmel Live! guest host Colman Domingo has some thoughts. "Now Elon Musk is planning to create what he calls a 'historically accurate' version of The Odyssey, using his Grok AI," Domingo says in the clip above. And I don't wanna get technical here, but you can't make a historically accurate version of something that is totally made the f*** up. "I mean, The Odyssey is fiction. You know, like The Hunger Games, or the president's cognitive exams. I mean come on, doesn't Elon Musk have better things to do? Wasn't this bitch supposed to be on Mars by now?"
